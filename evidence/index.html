05 Limits

What this does not prove.

An honest record names its edges:

  • It is not hardware validation. Suspend, radios, audio, ports and the rest are field evidence for one model, not a compatibility claim.
  • It is not accessibility E2E. The session check exercises the desktop through the session, not through assistive APIs.
  • It is not bit-for-bit reproducible. The plan and the Containerfile are deterministic; the image build is not yet.
  • It says nothing about RHEL. RHEL-derived images are built privately and never published.
